Viganj

Wind, watersports and a relaxed waterfront atmosphere.

Day trip from Orebić

Viganj lies west of Orebić and is one of the Adriatic’s best-known windsurfing and kitesurfing spots. Even without watersports, it is worth visiting for beaches, views and a laid-back pace.

How to plan the day

Viganj is a short coastal drive from Sunny Day.

Sunny Day is an excellent base for exploring Pelješac, nearby islands and southern Dalmatia. For transport, boat connections and weather conditions, check current information shortly before travelling.

Quick tips

  • Start earlier in the day.
  • Bring water and sun protection.
  • Check seasonal connections and opening times.
