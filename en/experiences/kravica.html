Kravica Waterfalls

A dramatic waterfall landscape in Herzegovina for a full-day excursion.

Day trip from Orebić

Kravica Waterfalls are in Bosnia and Herzegovina, known for a broad tufa cascade and green surroundings. The trip suits guests who want a nature-focused day inland.

How to plan the day

Because this trip crosses an international border, check travel-document and entry requirements before departure.

Sunny Day is an excellent base for exploring Pelješac, nearby islands and southern Dalmatia. For transport, boat connections and weather conditions, check current information shortly before travelling.

Check accommodation availability

Quick tips

  • Start earlier in the day.
  • Bring water and sun protection.
  • Check seasonal connections and opening times.
  • For Kravica, carry the required travel documents.
