Split, Trogir, Omiš & Cetina

The longest trip on the list, but excellent for a longer stay: ancient Split, Trogir, Omiš and the Cetina canyon.

Why go

Split, Trogir, Omiš & Cetina

For guests who want to discover even more of Dalmatia, a full-day trip to Split, Trogir and Omiš combines history, the sea and impressive landscapes. In Split, explore Diocletian’s Palace and the famous Riva, while Trogir charms with its preserved historic centre and stone streets. Omiš offers a completely different experience with fortresses, dramatic cliffs and the Cetina River canyon.

How to plan the day

For one day, choose no more than two main areas, such as Split + Omiš/Cetina or Split + Trogir. Trying to cover everything would turn the day into too much driving.
