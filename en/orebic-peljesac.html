Orebić & Pelješac

Sea, wine, mountains and islands in one place.

The Mediterranean at an easier pace

Orebić lies on the sunny southern side of the Pelješac peninsula, directly opposite Korčula. It combines beaches, maritime heritage and a waterfront lifestyle with an excellent base for day trips.

Pelješac wine roads

Pelješac is known for Plavac Mali, the Dingač and Postup wine areas and family-run wineries. A wine day pairs naturally with Ston, panoramic drives and local food.

Active days

Viganj and Kučište are famous for wind and watersports, while Mount St. Ilija rises dramatically above Orebić. For a slower day, stay close to the beaches, pool and promenade.
