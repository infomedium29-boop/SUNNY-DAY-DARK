GDPR & Your Rights

Clear information about how we protect your personal data.

GDPR — data subject rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you specific rights in relation to personal data we process.

Your rights

Right of access

You may ask whether we process your personal data and request a copy of the data concerning you.

Right to rectification

You may request correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete data.

Right to erasure

In certain circumstances you may request deletion of your data, unless a legal obligation or another valid legal basis requires continued retention.

Right to restriction

You may request temporary restriction of processing in the circumstances provided by the GDPR.

Right to data portability

Where applicable, you may request your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

Right to object

Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Withdrawal of consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

How to exercise your rights

You can submit a request using the contact details published on the Kapcsolat page. To protect your privacy, we may ask for reasonable proof of identity before acting on a request.

Requests are handled without undue delay and within the time limits prescribed by the GDPR.

Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe your personal data is being processed unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Croatia, this is the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

Related information

For details about what data we collect, why we process it and how long it is retained, see our Adatvédelmi szabályzat.
